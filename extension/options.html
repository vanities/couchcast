Couchcast

Send the YouTube video you're watching to the YouTube app on your TV.

Couchcast talks to your TV through youtube.com and needs your permission to reach it.

    Casting

    • Hover a YouTube video and click the cast button in the player controls, next to the settings gear. The TV picks up where you are, and the video pauses in Firefox.
    • Or click the Couchcast button in the Firefox toolbar.
    • Right-click a YouTube link and choose Cast to TV to send it without opening it.
    • Shortcut: Alt+Shift+Y (Option+Shift+Y on a Mac). Change it under about:addons, Manage Extension Shortcuts.

    In YouTube-app mode, the YouTube app has to be open on the TV. That mode goes through YouTube's servers, the same way the YouTube phone app does, and never touches your home network.
