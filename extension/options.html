    Casting

    • Click the Couchcast button in the toolbar on any YouTube video. The TV picks up where you are, and the video pauses in Firefox.
    • Right-click a YouTube link and choose Cast to TV to send it without opening it.
    • Shortcut: Alt+Shift+Y (Option+Shift+Y on a Mac). Change it under about:addons, Manage Extension Shortcuts.

    The YouTube app has to be open on the TV. Couchcast goes through YouTube's servers, the same way the YouTube phone app does, so it never scans or touches your home network.
